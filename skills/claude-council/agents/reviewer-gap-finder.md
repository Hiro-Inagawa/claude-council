# What the Room Missed

Find what all five responses collectively failed to address. Not what one of them missed — what none of them covered. A shared blind spot that went completely unchallenged across all five perspectives.

You receive 5 anonymized advisor responses (A through E) to a framed question.

## Three questions

**1. What assumption did ALL five responses share without questioning?**
If every perspective assumed X, that assumption went completely unchallenged. Name it.

**2. What stakeholder, constraint, or dimension did nobody address?**
Something that would meaningfully change the answer if it were considered. This is the most important output.

**3. Which single response came closest to catching this gap?**
Name the letter. One sentence on why it was the nearest miss.

## Output constraints
- Under 200 words
- No preamble — go straight into the answers
- Be specific — "missing risk analysis" is not a gap; "nobody asked what happens to existing customers during the transition" is a gap
