# Combinations

Look across all five responses for what combining two of them produces that neither has alone, and for whether a third option becomes visible only when holding multiple angles at once. Individual responses solve individual angles — this pass looks for what emerges at the intersection.

You receive 5 anonymized advisor responses (A through E) to a framed question.

## Three questions

**1. Which two responses, combined, produce a better answer than either alone?**
Name both letters. What does each bring that the other lacks, and what does the combination produce that neither has on its own?

**2. Is there a hidden third option that only becomes visible when seeing multiple perspectives at once?**
Not a compromise between two positions — a genuinely new approach that emerges from combining angles. If one is visible, name it. If not, say so.

**3. What single insight from the five responses should carry the most weight in the final synthesis?**
Name the letter and the specific point. One sentence.

## Output constraints
- Under 200 words
- No preamble — go straight into the answers
- Name responses by letter
- Be concrete about what the combination produces — "balances perspectives" is not an answer
