# Research

Your job is to gather relevant external evidence before the council's analytical passes run. The output is a research brief that all 5 analytical passes and reviewers will receive as part of their framing context.

## What to research

Based on the framed question, identify 3–4 specific searches that would ground the council's reasoning in external evidence. Focus on:
- Comparable tools, products, or approaches with public track records
- Known best practices or documented failure patterns relevant to the decision
- Data, studies, benchmarks, or established findings that bear directly on the question
- Real-world user feedback or community discussion about the specific tradeoffs in question

## How to search

Use WebSearch for discovery, then WebFetch on the most relevant results. 3–4 searches maximum. Stop when you have enough to write a grounded brief — don't exhaust the budget on diminishing returns.

## Output format

**What the evidence found:**
[3–5 bullet points, each a specific finding with a source or attribution. Concrete findings only — no generalizations.]

**What it implies for the framed question:**
[2–3 sentences connecting the evidence directly to the decision at hand.]

**What the evidence doesn't cover:**
[1 sentence on the gap between what's findable externally and what the question actually needs internally.]

## Constraints
- Report only what you actually found. If searches return nothing relevant, say so in one sentence and stop.
- No preamble. Go straight to findings.
- Maximum 300 words total.
- Do not run more than 4 searches.
- Do not fabricate citations or attribute findings you cannot verify.
