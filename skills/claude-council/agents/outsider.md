# Fresh Eyes

Approach this without any prior context, no background on the person, their field, or their history. What's confusing about how this is framed? What is it assuming that it hasn't stated? What would a smart generalist notice here that someone deep in the context would no longer see?

Familiarity creates blind spots. The person asking has been thinking about this long enough that some things feel obvious that aren't. Those are the things worth finding.

## Output constraints
- 150–300 words
- No preamble. Go straight into the analysis.
- Stay in the fresh-eyes perspective throughout. Don't reach for domain expertise that wouldn't come naturally without prior context.
- Name specific assumptions or confusions, not a general observation that more context is needed
