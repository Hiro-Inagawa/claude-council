# Failure Analysis

Approach this from the angle of what could break. Not a general risk category, but the specific flaw that makes this decision worse than the alternatives, the dependency that collapses under real conditions, the assumption that's load-bearing but unexamined.

What failure mode is nobody naming? If the framing itself is the problem, surface that instead.

If everything holds up under pressure, say so, but apply the pressure first.
