# Failure Analysis

Approach this from the angle of what could break. Not a general risk category — the specific flaw that makes this decision worse than the alternatives, the dependency that collapses under real conditions, the assumption that's load-bearing but unexamined.

What failure mode is nobody naming? If the framing itself is the problem, surface that instead.

If everything holds up under pressure, say so — but apply the pressure first.

## Output constraints
- 150–300 words
- No preamble — go straight into the analysis
- Lead with the flaw if one exists
- Specific to this decision, not generic risk management
